CASE STUDY 01 / 03Fashion · Clothing brand

Naima Design

A dedicated online presence for a clothing brand, built to give the brand a clear place to present its identity and collections to potential customers.

The business need

A clothing brand needs more than a social profile. It needs a consistent online destination where visitors can understand the brand, explore what it offers and find a straightforward way to get in touch.

Raazim Tech's approach

We created a branded website experience focused on presenting Naima Design clearly, keeping the visual identity at the centre and making the experience usable across screen sizes.

What the website is designed to support

  • A dedicated home for the brand's online identity.
  • Clear presentation of clothing and brand content.
  • Responsive browsing across phones, tablets and desktop screens.
  • A direct path for interested visitors to contact the business.

Project outcome

Naima Design now has a dedicated website that gives the brand an additional digital touchpoint beyond social media. This case study describes the website deliverable, not independently measured sales or conversion results.

CASE STUDY 02 / 03Transport · Passenger travel

Star Bus Somaliland

A transport website that helps passengers explore routes, understand the service and start a booking conversation directly with the company.

The business need

Passengers need quick access to practical travel information before they make a journey: where the company travels, what to expect and how to contact the operator.

Raazim Tech's approach

We built a public-facing website that brings the company's routes, service information and contact options into one place, with WhatsApp used as a direct booking and enquiry path.

Key website features

  • Destination information for Hargeisa, Borama and Wajaale.
  • Route and service details presented in clear sections.
  • WhatsApp booking links that help visitors start an enquiry.
  • Daily service hours and customer contact information.
  • Responsive layouts for mobile and desktop visitors.

Project outcome

The website gives Star Bus a public online destination for travel information and a direct digital route to customer enquiries. Search Console history previously shared for this project showed meaningful impressions and clicks, but those figures are not presented here as current live performance.

CASE STUDY 03 / 03Hospitality · Cafe

Ardaa Cafe

A welcoming cafe website that brings the menu, atmosphere and ordering path together in one simple experience.

The business need

Customers should be able to get a feel for the cafe, browse popular menu items, find its location and understand how to place an order without searching through scattered information.

Raazim Tech's approach

We redesigned the website around a warm cafe identity, retaining Ardaa Cafe's original logo and using a clear menu-led structure with direct WhatsApp ordering.

Key website features

  • Menu presentation with category filters and featured items.
  • WhatsApp ordering links from menu items and calls to action.
  • Location information with a map section.
  • English and Somali language switching.
  • Responsive navigation and menu cards for mobile visitors.

Project outcome

Ardaa Cafe has a dedicated online menu and a simpler path for customers to discover the cafe and send an order through WhatsApp. The original cafe logo was retained as requested.
