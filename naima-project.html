Fashion · Website project

Naima Design

A dedicated website for a clothing brand, presenting its identity and creative work through a responsive online presence.

The business need

A clothing brand needs a consistent online destination where visitors can understand the brand, explore its offer and find a direct way to get in touch.

Our approach

We created a branded website experience focused on presenting Naima Design clearly, keeping the visual identity central and making the experience usable across screen sizes.

Key website features

  • Dedicated online home for the brand
  • Clear presentation of clothing and brand content
  • Responsive browsing across phones, tablets and desktop
  • Direct contact path for interested visitors

Project outcome

Naima Design has a dedicated website that gives the brand an additional digital touchpoint beyond social media.
