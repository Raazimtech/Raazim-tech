NVIDIA board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

The board's decision returns NVIDIA's co-founder to the top role while the company maintains an independent investigation and adds new national-security and export-control oversight.

Raazim Tech Editorial DeskPublished 24 November 2026 · 10:54 EAT3 min read

NVIDIA announced on November 23 that its board had reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as president and chief executive officer, effective immediately. The company also confirmed that Huang returned to the board of directors.

A filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission records the board action on November 22. NVIDIA said the board voted unanimously after discussions with Huang and input from senior U.S. government officials.

What NVIDIA announced

Huang resumes the president and CEO roles. Colette Kress continues as NVIDIA's executive vice president and chief financial officer, while Harvey Jones remains chair of the board.

The company said an independent committee formed to investigate recent reporting involving Huang will continue its work through completion. NVIDIA also announced a separate National Security and Export Control Committee made up of independent directors to oversee related risks.

Additional compliance oversight

NVIDIA says the new committee is engaging legal counsel with national-security experience and that the company is beginning a search for a chief compliance officer. The role is intended to oversee compliance across the company and report to the board's Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.

The board's announcement should not be read as saying that the independent investigation or ongoing government inquiries have concluded. NVIDIA said it will continue to cooperate with government investigations and inquiries.

Why the leadership decision matters

NVIDIA is a central supplier in the global AI computing ecosystem. Leadership decisions, export compliance and governance can affect how customers, partners and regulators assess the company. The reinstatement restores Huang to the top executive role, while the continuing investigation and new oversight structures remain important developments to follow.

Raazim Tech takeaway

There are two separate developments: a leadership decision that is effective immediately, and governance and compliance work that NVIDIA says is still ongoing. Keeping those facts distinct is important when following the story.

What to watch next

Watch for updates from the independent investigation, the new board committee, the chief compliance officer search and any further disclosures in regulatory filings.
