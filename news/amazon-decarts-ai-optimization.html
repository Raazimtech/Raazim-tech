Amazon agrees to acquire Decart's AI optimization technology

The agreement focuses on Decart's optimization technology and team, which Amazon says could help improve performance across its custom AI chips and inference systems.

Raazim Tech Editorial DeskPublished 24 November 2026 · 10:54 EAT3 min read

Amazon says it has signed an agreement to purchase the proprietary technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and bring Decart's co-founders, AI researchers and engineers into Amazon. The company announced the agreement on November 23, describing it as part of its effort to improve custom silicon and AI model performance.

Amazon's announcement describes a technology-and-talent transaction. It does not say that Amazon is acquiring every part of Decart or all of its products.

What is Decart Optimization Stack?

Decart Optimization Stack is software designed to improve how AI models run across computing hardware. Amazon says the technology is intended to help optimize both inference and custom silicon performance. Inference is the stage where a trained model processes new input and generates an output.

Amazon says Decart has worked with the company since 2025 on performance for its Trainium chips. Trainium is part of Amazon's custom silicon portfolio for AI workloads. The companies describe their work as a full-stack effort across hardware and software.

Why Amazon is interested

AI infrastructure competition is not only about building faster chips. The software stack that schedules work, compiles models and uses hardware resources also affects throughput, latency and operating cost. Improving that layer could make it easier for customers to train and run models on Amazon's own processors.

For cloud providers, better performance per unit of compute can help make their infrastructure more attractive to developers and businesses. Amazon says the proposed purchase is intended to advance that goal, although the actual customer impact will depend on integration and measured performance after the transaction closes.

What is confirmed about the agreement?

  • Amazon says it has signed an agreement to purchase the proprietary technology behind Decart Optimization Stack.
  • Decart's co-founders and a team of AI researchers and engineers are expected to join Amazon.
  • Amazon says the companies have collaborated on Trainium performance since 2025.
  • Amazon did not disclose financial terms in its announcement.
  • Completion is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory clearance.
Raazim Tech takeaway

The story is about the strategic importance of AI software optimization. Hardware matters, but getting more useful work from each chip is also a major part of the economics of AI infrastructure.

What to watch next

Watch for details about how the technology is integrated into Amazon's AI services, whether developers see measurable performance improvements on Trainium, and whether Amazon provides further information about the transaction after regulatory review.
