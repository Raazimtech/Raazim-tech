How we cover technology news

Our goal is to make technical and business developments easier to understand without overstating what is known.

  • We prioritize original company announcements, public filings and official documentation.
  • We link to primary sources and use established reporting for additional context.
  • We distinguish confirmed facts from company projections, reported estimates and pending approvals.
  • We show publication dates and correct material errors when they are identified.
